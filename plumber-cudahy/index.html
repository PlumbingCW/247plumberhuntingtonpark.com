The hot water heater problem Cudahy callers describe most

A heater that runs lukewarm or spits rusty water is often near the end, especially in a rental where nobody has flushed it. A leak from the tank body means water heater repair cannot fix the corrosion inside, and a replacement is the straight answer. A leaking fitting, valve or element is a repair.

Ask your landlord who owns the heater before you buy one. Tell the plumber what fuel it uses, since gas and electric tanks need different parts. For gas, leave the area and call SoCalGas right away if you smell it.
