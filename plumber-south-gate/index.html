Well water at 226 mg/L and your water heater

South Gate Public Works supplies local deep groundwater. The city's 2021 water quality report lists total hardness averaging 226.3 mg/L, about 13 grains per gallon, which the USGS scale calls very hard. That kind of water leaves scale on heater elements and burners and stiffens fixtures over time.

A tank that has never been flushed tends to get noisy and fail sooner. When a heater starts to leak from the tank itself, repair rarely helps, and water heater replacement is the realistic fix. California law also requires bracing under Health and Safety Code 19211. Readings differ by tap, so test yours, and call Public Works with water quality questions because we make no health claims.
