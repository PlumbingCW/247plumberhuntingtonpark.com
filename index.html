When plumbing cannot wait

A pipe that has let go does damage by the minute, and a sewer backup is a health problem as much as a plumbing one. Our emergency line is open at night, on weekends and on holidays, because that is when these problems tend to show up.

The steps below are what we would tell you on the phone. They buy time while a plumber gets to you.

  1. 1
    Shut off the waterTurn the main valve clockwise, usually at the meter box near the street or on the front wall of the house. Open a low faucet to drain the lines.
  2. 2
    Protect what you canMove rugs and electronics, switch off power to wet areas at the breaker, and photograph the damage for your records.
  3. 3
    Smell gas? Leave firstWalk out, avoid switches and flames, and call SoCalGas or 911 from outside before you call us.
  4. 4
    Call the 24/7 lineTell us what you see and where the valve is. We will line up a technician and explain the next step.

Local water, local wear

The City of Huntington Park 2025 Consumer Confidence Report gives these averages. The US Geological Survey calls anything above 180 mg/L very hard, so every tap in the city sits in that band.

Water sourceAverage total hardnessAbout in grains per gallon
City groundwater wells214 mg/L12.5 gpg
Metropolitan Water District supply235 mg/L13.7 gpg

Very hard water leaves scale on water heater elements and burners and shortens the life of fixtures. That is why we flush tanks and tankless units, check water heater repair causes early, and size a water softener with the real number. Blends vary by tap and season, so test your own water before buying equipment.

Housing matters too. The city is about 3 square miles, one of the densest in the state, and nearly built out. Older lines sit close together under tight lots, which makes a camera look worth doing before any digging.
