A line between meter and house: repair it or replace it

A leak in the buried line between the meter and the house shows up as a wet strip of yard, a sudden high bill or low pressure with no visible water. We find it with acoustic ground microphones and tracer gas, then scope the cheaper options before any trench is opened.

A single failed joint can be repaired. A line that has leaked more than once, or that is old galvanized steel, is usually better replaced in one run, and water line repair and replacement is where that comparison is laid out. In a city this tightly built, ask how the plumber plans to protect the neighbor's side of the fence and any shared walls.
